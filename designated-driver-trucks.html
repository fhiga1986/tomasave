The service

What is a designated driver for trucks?

A professional driver who takes care of driving your truck or heavy cargo unit when your regular driver is not available, or when you need to move the unit without slowing your operation. The SAVE driver arrives at the point you indicate, takes charge of the unit and drives it to its destination.

SAVE does not rent trucks and is not a transport company: we provide the driver, you provide the unit. The unit and the operation stay yours, and you decide when and for how long you need support.

Units we drive

  • Trucks and dump trucks
  • Heavy cargo units
  • Company and fleet units
  • Units moving to a workshop or yard

Frequently asked questions

Questions about a designated driver for trucks

Does SAVE rent trucks or operate as a transport company?

No. SAVE provides the driver service: we drive your own unit. We do not rent vehicles or supply the truck.

What is a Driver A3C?

It is the SAVE driver category for heavy transport units. When we quote, we confirm the driver that matches your unit.

Do you operate in Lima and in the provinces?

Yes. We operate in Lima, Callao and the provinces. The cost depends on the route, the distance and the duration of the service.

Can I hire by the hour or for a full day?

Yes. You can hire per trip, by the hour or by the day. For recurring needs, we arrange a scheduled service.

Do you issue invoices and which payment methods do you accept?

Yes. We issue electronic receipts and invoices. We accept Yape, Plin and BCP bank transfer.

Are you available 24 hours?

Yes. SAVE is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Message us on WhatsApp and we will coordinate availability.
